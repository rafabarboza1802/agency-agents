# Loop 2 — Weekly Bid Pipeline

**Cadence:** weekly (suggested Monday) + ad hoc when a bid is due inside 7 days · **Run by:** BC Chief of Staff

## Roster

| Agent | Role this week |
|-------|----------------|
| BC Public Bid Hunter | Bid calendar, compliance matrices, addenda tracking, go/no-go inputs |
| BC Pipeline and Account Manager | Pipeline review, win-probability, stage hygiene, follow-up plan |
| BC Estimator | Estimates for every GO; unit-cost library updates from closed jobs |
| BC Proposal and Bid Writer | Draft proposals, bid packages, sub quotes |
| BC Finance and Job Costing Analyst | Bond/backlog check, cash-flow effect of bidding & winning, margin floor |
| BC Safety and Cal-OSHA Advisor | Safety-driven cost and qualification items (EMR, safety program, special hazards) |
| BC Construction Project Manager | Capacity and schedule reality check |
| BC Commercial Outreach Rep | Follow-up drafts on outstanding proposals |

## Agenda (the Chief of Staff produces this as one weekly packet)

1. **Bid calendar** — every tracked opportunity with: due date/time, submission method, mandatory pre-bid / site walk, RFI deadline, bond %, status, owner agent.
2. **Go / No-go decisions needed** — one card per opportunity: scope fit, licence/DIR/bond/insurance check against the profile, capacity, competition, effort vs. expected value, recommendation. → **approver decides.**
3. **Estimates in progress** — % complete, open questions, the number the Estimator is converging on (internal only).
4. **Proposals ready for approval** — price, exclusions, compliance matrix status. → **approver approves price (G4) and send/submit (G2/G3)** per item.
5. **Outstanding proposals** — age, last touch, drafted follow-up. → approver approves follow-ups (G1).
6. **Results** — bid tabs for public openings, wins/losses with reason and price gap.
7. **Capacity & cash** — backlog vs. crews, bonded backlog vs. aggregate limit, cash-flow effect of likely wins.
8. **Profile gaps** — `TBD` facts that blocked a bid this week (e.g. bonding limit, DIR number), with who can fix them.

## Submission-day checklist (Public Bid Hunter + Proposal Writer)

- [ ] All addenda acknowledged on the bid form
- [ ] Bid bond / cashier's check prepared (amount = required %)
- [ ] Subcontractor listing complete (CA Subletting & Subcontracting Fair Practices Act, Pub. Contract Code §4100 et seq. — listing threshold per the solicitation)
- [ ] DIR registration number entered; prevailing-wage acknowledgement signed
- [ ] Non-collusion declaration, other agency forms signed by an authorized signer
- [ ] SBE/DBE/DVBE participation forms or good-faith-effort documentation
- [ ] Price checked twice against the approved number (words and figures)
- [ ] Submission method tested (portal login, file size, deadline time zone)
- [ ] **Approver's explicit "submit" recorded** → then submit → save confirmation

## Weekly metrics

Bids submitted · hit rate (count and $) · average spread to low bid · bid cost (hours) per win · pipeline $ by stage · bonded backlog vs. limit.
