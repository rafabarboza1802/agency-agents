# Loop 3 — Active Project Delivery

**Cadence:** daily per active job (field) + weekly per job (management) + monthly (billing) · **Run by:** BC Chief of Staff, with BC Construction Project Manager as day-to-day owner

## Roster

| Agent | Responsibility |
|-------|----------------|
| BC Construction Project Manager | Schedule, daily logs, RFIs, submittals, change orders, subs, punch list |
| BC Safety and Cal-OSHA Advisor | Pre-task hazard review, toolbox talk, inspection checklist, incident documentation |
| BC Finance and Job Costing Analyst | Cost to date, budget vs. actual, pay apps, retention, AR, cash flow |
| BC Pipeline and Account Manager | Client relationship, upsell/next-job signals, review & referral at closeout |
| BC Property Management and HOA Lead Hunter | On PM/HOA jobs: other properties in the account with the same need (recurring-account expansion) |
| BC Commercial Outreach Rep | Drafts client-facing updates (approved before sending) |

## Daily (per job)

1. **Morning:** PM drafts the day plan (crew, equipment, deliveries, inspections). Safety drafts the toolbox talk and pre-task hazard checklist for that day's work.
2. **End of day:** PM compiles the daily log from crew input (work done, quantities, hours, weather, visitors, delays, photos). Finance codes hours/equipment/materials to cost codes.
3. **Flags raised immediately to the Chief of Staff:** injury or near miss, stop-work condition, utility strike, unforeseen condition (possible change order), inspection failure, owner directive outside scope.

## Weekly (per job)

- Schedule update (look-ahead 2 weeks) and critical-path risks
- RFI and submittal log — anything overdue
- Change-order log: potential COs → priced (Estimator) → drafted → **approved by approver** → sent to owner/GC → executed
- Budget vs. actual by cost code; projected final cost and margin
- Safety summary: inspections done, corrective actions open, training due
- Client update email **draft** (approved before sending)

## Monthly / per billing cycle

- Pay application / progress invoice drafted against schedule of values (Finance) with conditional progress lien releases as required
- Retention tracking per contract
- AR aging review and drafted reminders (approved before sending)
- Cash-flow forecast update across all jobs

## Closeout

Punch list → final inspection → closeout package (as-builts, warranties, O&M, CalGreen diversion records, certified payroll complete) → final pay app + retention request → final lien releases → estimate-vs-actual review fed back to the Estimator → review/referral request drafted for approval.

## Project metrics

Schedule variance · cost variance by cost code · CO turnaround days · RFI response days · recordable incidents / near misses reported · days sales outstanding · retention outstanding.
